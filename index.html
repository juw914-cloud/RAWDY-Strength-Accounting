How the counting works
Total strength always shows your platoon's own roster figure — it never moves. Any change is shown in red beside it (e.g. −1 out +2 in) so the difference is visible at a glance.

Move out and participating do reflect the attachments, because those are the numbers you actually march with.

Move out = total held, minus stay-backs (MC, SBF, MA, RSO, RSI, Duty, leave, course).
Participating = move out, minus those moving but not participating (safety walker, conducting staff, etc).

A few appointments carry a status by themselves — ASA and SAGE move out but do not participate, and MPAT is attached out of the coy. Tagging the man overrides it, so it is only a default.

Pull a man into another platoon and he leaves his own platoon's figures and joins theirs — with his gear. Because that draws on someone else's strength, the tool asks first and shows both platoons' figures, and the receiving platoon keeps a green note saying how many of its pax are accounted from elsewhere.

Attached out means he goes out for training, but under another coy or unit — so he is no longer on your move out or your participating strength. If he is attached to another coy, the company total drops as well, because he is not ours for this activity. If he is attached to one of our own platoons, he simply counts under them instead and the company total is unchanged.

MC, SBF, MA and Duty stay in camp, so they come off move out and participating too. The Strength accounting panel on each platoon tab lists every one of these deductions by name, so you can trace the figures from roster strength down to participating.

An attachment is a single record shared by both platoons: remove it from either side and both restore instantly. Within the company, his weapon and gear move with him — the platoon he is attached to counts his equipment, so every PS's end state is exactly what is physically inside his platoon. Attached out to another coy/unit, his gear still counts at home (drawn from our armskote), unless his gear source is set to theirs from his name.

Status categories — add, remove, customise
Every status a man can be tagged with, and how each behaves. Applies to all platoons. Out of camp = MC / MA / leave / course (off strength, no gear). In camp, not moving = SBF / duty (off move-out, no gear). Moves out, no participate, no gear = safety walker / conducting staff (they move out but don't train and draw no gear).
